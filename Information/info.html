Hello, Welcome to Quiz Game

Welcome to the ultimate challenge for curious minds!
This quiz game gives you the opportunity to test your knowledge
across three exciting categories: Animals, History, and Geography & Maps.
Each category contains a variety of questions designed to
challenge what you know and help you discover interesting new facts along the way.

Animals

Think you know the animal kingdom? Test your knowledge of
creatures from all around the world! Answer questions about
mammals, birds, reptiles, insects, marine animals, and much more.
From the fastest animals on Earth to the deepest creatures in the ocean,
you'll discover just how amazing the natural world can be.

History

Take a journey through time and explore the events that helped
shape our world. Challenge yourself with questions about ancient
civilizations, famous leaders, important discoveries, major wars, inventions,
and historical moments. Whether you're interested in ancient Egypt or more recent
world events, this category will put your knowledge of the past to the test.

Map

How well do you know the world around you? Test your knowledge of
countries, capitals, continents, oceans, landmarks, borders, and famous locations.
Explore different parts of the globe and see if you can identify important places and
geographical facts from around the world.

Are you ready

Choose your favorite category and begin the challenge! Answer each question
carefully and try to earn the highest score possible. When you're finished, check the
Score Board to see how well you did.
